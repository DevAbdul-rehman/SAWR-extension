# Privacy Policy for SAWR (Smart AI Website Reader)

**Last updated: October 2026**

SAWR ("the Extension") is a client-side browser extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. **Data Collection and Storage:** The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers owned by the developer. All settings, chat history, and API keys are stored locally on your device using browser storage.
2. **Third-Party Services:** The Extension transmits the text of your actively viewed web pages to the Google Gemini API solely for the purpose of generating summaries and answers. This data is subject to Google's API privacy policies.
3. **Permissions:** The extension requires 'activeTab' and 'scripting' to read the page you want summarized, and 'storage' to save your local chat history.
